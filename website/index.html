What Solvex Audio does

Simulation

Sealed, vented, passive-radiator and 4th-order bandpass boxes, plus tapped and folded horns in Pro. See frequency response, cone excursion, port air velocity and impedance change as you adjust the design.

Box and cutting list

Enter the volume you want and your panel thickness. Solvex works out the box, subtracts the port volume and lists every panel with its size. Home exports the panels as DXF or SVG for a CNC or CAD program.

Fit checks

Warns you when the magnet hits the back panel, the driver is too deep for the box, or a port ends too close to a wall. In Home you can enter your trunk size and see straight away whether the box fits.

Ports

Round and slot ports, and 90° bent ports in Home. The port air velocity graph shows where the port will start to chuff, so you can make it bigger before you build it.

Calculators

Speaker wiring and impedance, Ohm's law, wire size, battery bank size, dB/SPL and cone area comparison. There's also a tone generator for finding rattles and air leaks in a finished box.

The basics

Windows, macOS and Linux. Metric or imperial units. English, Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, German, French and Spanish. A built-in driver library, plus CSV import for your own drivers.

Questions

How does the open beta work?

During the open beta every feature is unlocked, free of charge. You don't need an account or payment details. When the beta ends, Basic stays free and Home and Pro can be bought under Prices.

Can I use Solvex Audio offline?

Yes. Your licence is stored on your computer, so Solvex runs without internet for up to 72 hours at a time. After that it needs a quick connection to check the licence.

Does it run on a Mac?

Yes, natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The download is a zip with the app inside: unzip it and drag Solvex Audio into Applications. The beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click the app, choose Open, then confirm.

How do I add my own drivers?

Open the Drivers tab and click Import CSV. Solvex also comes with a driver library, grouped by brand.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

In the app, choose Help → Report a Problem…. It opens our support form with your version and system filled in. If you leave your email, we'll reply.
