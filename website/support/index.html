Support & bug reports

Found a bug, got stuck, or have an idea for Solvex Audio? Tell us here. Every report is read, and if you leave your email we'll get back to you.

Tip: in the app, Help → Report a Problem… opens this form with your version and system already filled in.

What is this about?

Please don't include passwords, licence keys or payment details. Reports are stored securely and deleted after two years. See the Privacy Policy.

Before you write

  • Check the FAQ. It covers installing on macOS and Linux, offline use and importing drivers.
  • Make sure you have the latest version: see the changelog, or use Help → Check for Updates… in the app.
  • Simulation results are estimates. See the Terms of Use for what Solvex can and can't promise.
